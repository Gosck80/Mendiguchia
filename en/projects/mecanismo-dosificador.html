The problem

Dosing spices without shaking the jar

Perforated lids force you to shake the jar, over-dose and let cooking steam in. The proposal is a cap with an Archimedes screw that delivers a fixed dose for every 120° turn and closes when released.

It fits the existing packaging line, with the same thread and the same jar, and is designed in mono-material rPET. An asymmetric hopper with tooth diffusers keeps the powder from bridging.

Ideation

From sketch to Archimedes screw

Gates and gravity valves were explored, but they jammed and took up too much space. The first schemes already set the free-turning traditional grinder against a thread-limited doser.

The screw solved both the exact volume and the spill barrier in minimal space, and fitted inside the cap without changing the brand’s jar.

Engineering

Dimensioned drawings

Cap and screw snap together: a pivot at the end of the shaft fits a blind hole moulded into the cap, with no extra fasteners.

The ratchet returns to its locked position through the material’s own elasticity. General tolerance ±0.10 mm, tighter on the shaft–hole fit.

Prototyping and testing

Iterate until it flows and holds

Printed prototypes to eliminate product compaction: asymmetric funnel and a calibrated outlet. The final PET-G version works with real product and fits the current jar range.
