Ideation

Shell sketches

First freehand explorations: continuous oval volumes seen from above, in profile and in perspective, with a line running around the body that splits the two halves of the shell.

In the centre, a layout scheme: central body, arms and rotors. The perimeter line from the sketches returns in the final design as the light band.

Aerodynamic design

Helical propellers and an aerodynamic shell

Propulsion
Four rotors with three-blade helical propellers and a 3:1 thrust-to-weight ratio.
Shell
Aerodynamic continuous-body shell, with a single light band as its visual signature.
Goal
A drone concept built around stability, speed and agility.
