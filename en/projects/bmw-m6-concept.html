Ideation

One line that wraps the car

The exploration started from BMW’s identity: the futuristic flow of the i8, the muscular stance of the M cars and the elegance of the 7 Series. The core idea, one continuous line wrapping the car, was simplified to the rear section, joining the A-pillar with the spoiler.

Modelling and drawings

From sketch to parametric model

Parametric model in CATIA V5 with dimensions and reference geometry set from the start, organised in a tree by subgroups. Fill for simple surfaces such as the bonnet and Multi-section for the rear wing, with curvature and continuity control.

Renders · Blender

Light signature and setting

The illuminated kidney grille and the rear light define the car’s identity at night. Colour and scene tests in the city, on the road and on track.
