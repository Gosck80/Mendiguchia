The problem

There was no spare part

A component of the Peugeot 205 (1983–1998) heater module with no spare available. The solution is to recreate it: measure the original, model it completely and print a first prototype.

Design and fabrication

From calipers to first prototype

The part is fully modelled from measurements of the original, to ±0.05 mm. For printing, the orientation is chosen so the layers work in favour of strength.
